## Project-Workup-Workout
Lately, I have been contemplating dead projects and inertia. How hard is it to get a project moving again? Resolution, each day go through an exercise of spinning them all up and clicking 1 thing. This tells your brain the project matters and re-wires/orients your brain to what it does. It gives you human context. 🤪
